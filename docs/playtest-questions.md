## GDD §24 Emotional Quality Questions

These questions target the vertical slice's emotional thesis: belonging, curiosity, consequence, and forward momentum.

### Does the player understand why the caravan matters?

- **Prompt:** "Why do you think Kvothe travels with these people? What would he lose if they were gone?"
- **Observation criteria:** Player references family, safety, music, shared work, or Abenthy's mentorship rather than treating the caravan as a generic quest hub.
